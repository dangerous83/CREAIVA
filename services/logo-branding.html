Common Questions

FAQ

The questions we get most often about logo & branding.

How long does a full brand build take?
A typical engagement runs 5–6 weeks from kickoff to handoff. Rush timelines are possible when the strategy is already clear.
Do you provide the brand strategy too, or just the visuals?
Both. Every project starts with a positioning and audience workshop — the visuals follow the strategy, not the other way around.
What files will I receive?
Native design files (Figma, Illustrator), exported logos in every format (SVG, PNG, PDF, EPS, favicon set), a full guidelines PDF, and any custom fonts we license for you.
Can you match an existing product or website we already have?
Yes. When you have existing assets, we treat the brand refresh as an evolution — pulling forward what already works, replacing what doesn't.
