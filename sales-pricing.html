Logo & Branding

A complete brand identity system — not just a logo file. We build the mark, typography, color palette, and brand voice into a cohesive system with guidelines your client's team can follow across every touchpoint.

This service covers the full brand identity lifecycle. We start with a discovery phase where we audit the client's market position, competitors, and target audience to define brand positioning. Stationery deliverables include business card (front/back), letterhead (A4), envelope (DL), email signature template, and a basic PowerPoint/Keynote template. All files are delivered in AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, and PDF formats organized in a clearly labeled folder structure. The client gets full ownership of all assets upon final payment.

2D Animation

AED 550 – 7,700

Illustrated explainers, character animation, and stylized loops with real personality. Includes script, storyboard, and voice-over direction in Arabic or English.

Our 2D animation service covers the full production pipeline from concept to final render. We begin with scriptwriting — a clear, concise narrative (typically 150-180 words per minute of screen time) that distills the client's message into a compelling story. The storyboard phase translates the script into a visual shot-by-shot plan (typically 15-25 frames per minute of animation) showing composition, character poses, key transitions, and text placement. The design phase establishes the illustration style — we offer flat/minimal, isometric, hand-drawn, infographic, and character-driven styles. For character animation, we design custom characters based on client direction, build them with digital rigs (bone-based in After Effects or Toon Boom) for smooth, repeatable motion, and animate them with lip-sync if voice-over is involved. Voice-over production includes casting from our pool of professional Arabic and English voice artists, studio recording, and precise sync to animation. Sound design covers background music (royalty-free or custom-composed), sound effects, and audio mixing to broadcast-standard loudness (LUFS -14 for social, -24 for broadcast). Final delivery includes master file (4K or 1080p, ProRes or H.264), social media cutdowns (1:1, 9:16, 16:9), subtitled versions (hardcoded or SRT), and a GIF/thumbnail export for marketing use. Revision policy: 2-3 rounds included depending on tier, with changes tracked via a shared feedback document.

3D Animation

AED 2,500 – 14,000

Product visualization and 3D storytelling with cinematic lighting, texture, and detail. Photoreal renders, animated reveals, AR-ready assets (USDZ/GLB), and full CGI environments.

Our 3D pipeline covers modeling, texturing, lighting, animation, and rendering using industry-standard tools (Blender, Cinema 4D, or Maya depending on project needs). For product visualization, the client provides reference photos, CAD files, or physical samples — we build a high-poly 3D model, apply photorealistic PBR materials (metal, glass, fabric, plastic, etc.), set up HDRI-based lighting environments, and render at up to 8K resolution. Product renders include 5-8 beauty angles, hero composition shots, detail/macro views, and lifestyle context scenes. Turntable loops are 360-degree product spins (typically 5-8 seconds, 60-120 frames) delivered as MP4 and GIF. For animated reveals, we choreograph camera movements, product assembly/disassembly sequences, feature highlight callouts, and environment transitions — typically 30-60 seconds with sound design. AR-ready assets are exported in USDZ (Apple AR Quick Look) and GLB (Android/web AR) formats, optimized for mobile rendering with reduced polygon counts and baked textures while maintaining visual quality. Full CGI campaigns involve building custom 3D environments (architectural spaces, abstract worlds, product ecosystems), character modeling and animation, and rendering cinematic hero films at broadcast quality. All renders use physically-accurate global illumination and can match any specific lighting scenario (studio, outdoor, interior, dramatic). Delivery includes high-res stills (TIFF/PNG), video (ProRes/H.264), and source files on request.

SEO & Analytics

AED 1,100 – 2,500/mo

Technical SEO, content strategy, and analytics set up correctly from the start. GA4, Search Console, Meta Pixel setup, growth dashboards, and monthly reporting included.

Our SEO service is structured in two parts: an initial audit-and-setup phase (one-time), followed by an ongoing growth retainer (monthly). The technical SEO audit is a comprehensive review of the client's website covering: site speed analysis (Core Web Vitals — LCP, FID, CLS), crawlability assessment (robots.txt, XML sitemap, internal link structure, orphan pages), indexation review (Google Search Console coverage report, index bloat, duplicate content), mobile-first readiness (responsive design, tap targets, font sizes), schema markup audit (JSON-LD structured data for business, product, FAQ, article, breadcrumb, and local business types), and security (HTTPS, mixed content, HSTS headers). On-page optimization covers meta title and description optimization for target keywords (following Google's 60/160 character guidelines), heading hierarchy (H1-H6 structure), internal linking strategy with anchor text optimization, image optimization (alt text, WebP conversion, lazy loading), URL structure cleanup, and canonical tag implementation. Content strategy includes keyword research using Ahrefs/SEMrush (identifying 50-100 target keywords per quarter ranked by search volume, difficulty, and commercial intent), topic cluster mapping (pillar pages + supporting articles), content calendar with publishing cadence, and blog/landing page writing (if included in retainer). Analytics setup covers: GA4 property creation with enhanced measurement, custom event tracking (form submissions, button clicks, scroll depth, file downloads, video views), conversion goal setup, Google Search Console property verification and monitoring, Meta Pixel installation with standard events (ViewContent, Lead, Purchase), and Google Tag Manager container setup for clean tracking management. Growth dashboards are built in Looker Studio (formerly Google Data Studio) — a live, shareable dashboard showing traffic trends, keyword rankings, conversion rates, page performance, and channel attribution. Monthly reporting includes a 30-minute call reviewing performance, wins, issues, and next-month priorities, plus a written PDF report the client can share with their team.
